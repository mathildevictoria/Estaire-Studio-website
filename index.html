Social Media Management

For you if you want an active, welcoming social media presence without having to plan, create and remember to post every week.

I take care of your social media with a clear direction, consistent content and a personal approach.

Together, we build a presence that feels like your brand and gives people a reason to follow, connect and stay.

From turning your existing material into fresh content to publishing posts and responding to your community, I handle the day-to-day work so you can focus on your business.

You’ll also receive regular insights and reports, so you know what’s working, what we’re learning and what comes next.

Content Creation

Make more of the content you already have, and bring new ideas to life.

Content repurposing: I turn your webinars, newsletters, photos, videos and other existing material into social media posts and reels. This includes graphic design, photo editing and video editing, using content you already have or share with me along the way.

AI content: I use AI to expand on your existing visuals and create new product images, videos and campaign content with a realistic, high-end look that fits your brand.

Planning & Posting

I plan, schedule and publish your content, keeping your profiles active and consistent. Every post fits into a wider plan, with a recognisable look, tone and message across your social media.

Community Management

I respond to DMs, comments and other social media enquiries in your brand’s voice, helping your audience feel heard and keeping conversations going.

Brand Direction & Social Media Strategy

I review your current social media presence, get to know your business and audience, and analyse your competitors’ content. From there, I develop a clear strategy with content themes, a visual direction and a tone of voice that reflect your brand and support your goals.
